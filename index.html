Projects

Nigeria Import Trends & Economic Dependence 2010–2025

What does Nigeria import, from whom, and how does the naira affect it? An end-to-end pipeline on UN Comtrade and World Bank data.

  • Mineral fuels rose from 1.4% to 33.2% of imports; China supplied 29.4% in 2025.
  • The naira fell from ₦150 to ₦1,518 per US$, with no statistically significant link to import growth.
  • SQL database, 12 charts, 5-page dashboard, Power BI exports, 39 tests.

Nigeria's Crypto Adoption & Market Dynamics 2018–2026

How do Bitcoin and Ether behave for someone holding naira? Binance prices, CBN exchange rates, inflation and Google Trends in one validated dataset.

  • The Bitcoin "naira premium" (mean 32.9%) was almost entirely the gap between market and official FX rates.
  • Since the 2023 FX reform the exchange rate drives ~41% of weekly naira-return swings, up from ~1%.
  • HAC regressions, unit-root and break tests, event study; 6-page dashboard, 58 tests.
